GreenDesk: AI and Cloud-Enabled ESG Reporting

Governance & Compliance

RPA / AI consultant  ·  2026

< 1 minuteFor staff to report an incident
0/7 → 7/7LLM category accuracy after prompt redesign
Pilot, not launchEvidence-based go/no-go recommendation

The problem

A large organisation wanted to use AI and cloud automation to improve its ESG reporting, but it needed the solution to be safe, private, auditable and trustworthy.

My role

As consultant, I designed, built and tested the solution and gave the recommendation on whether it was ready to use.

What I did

  • Chatbot: built a Dialogflow CX assistant that captures an incident's type, location and risk in three questions, routes it to an urgent or standard pathway, and always offers a human handover. I tested four journeys and found and fixed a routing defect.
  • Governance by design: applied the Privacy Act 1988 (APPs) and Australia's AI Ethics Principles through data minimisation, a collection notice, an audit trail, human escalation for sensitive matters and WCAG 2.2 accessibility.
  • AI evaluation: compared LLM message triage with keyword and zero-shot baselines. The redesigned prompt was the only method to flag a critical safety case, but it also over-escalated and invented details.
  • Cloud risk: analysed the 2024 Snowflake customer breaches (about 165 organisations) using the shared-responsibility model, and set out preventive controls: MFA, network allow lists, credential rotation and monitoring.

Outcome and recommendation

The AI was not ready for production, so I recommended a human-in-the-loop pilot on Microsoft Azure, with safety overrides and human review of critical cases, and designed the Azure reference architecture. This is the kind of risk-based judgement that compliance and governance teams make.
